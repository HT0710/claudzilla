# Writing

Persisted text: docs, memory, commit and PR text, issues, Slack and other outside messages. Chat replies stay caveman; code comments follow `comments.md`.

- Reader first: what changed or what to do, then why. Not how; link the path instead.
- Every sentence adds something the reader doesn't already have.
- Brief, not vague: keep the facts that matter (names, numbers, paths); cut repeats and filler.
- Full sentences. Keep "because" and "so".
- Plain words a newcomer gets. No caveman shorthand: no arrows except plan `→ verify:` lines, no dropped articles, no internal nicknames.
- One idea per bullet or paragraph, under 40 words. If longer, split it.
- No em or en dashes as connectors; use a period, comma or colon.
- No parentheses inside parentheses. Make it its own sentence.
- One term per thing, the same one everywhere.
- No AI tells: `delve`, `seamless`, `robust`, `leverage`, `comprehensive`, `it's worth noting`.
